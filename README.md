## Synergy Shiny War 2026

Update `src/data/synergy-shiny-war-2026.json` with captains and player names matching the Shiny Showcase. Captains are included automatically, even if they are not in the players array. The page loads showcase catches from December 25, 2026 through January 8, 2027 (UTC) and currently uses SHOTM tier and trait scoring. Rosters rank players by their hit totals, and the hit list can be viewed together or by team. Team scores are the sum of their hits; power bars are relative to the leading score. Roster changes require a site rebuild and deployment.

The `exampleHits` array in that file contains illustrative hits and placeholder points included in scores. Remove the array or set it to `[]` to remove all examples; rebuild and deploy for the change to take effect.

The Shiny War cave background is based on [Ruakuri Cave by Pseudopanax](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stalactites,_stalagmites_and_columns_in_Ruakuri_Cave.jpg), released into the public domain by its author. The image was resized and converted to WebP.
